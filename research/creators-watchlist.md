**Background** (third-party profiles; not confirmed from a primary source):
- SEO and growth-marketing educator; reportedly held SEO roles at **Microsoft and Time**.
- Daily video and podcast habit: "1150+ days of marketing vids," "900+ days of The Edward Show" ([SuperX profile](https://superx.so/creators/edwardeachday)).
- The Edward Show is a daily digital-marketing podcast on YouTube with SEO guests ([Yoast event listing](https://yoast.com/about-us/events/the-edward-show-podcast/); [Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/92347/get-found-author-matt-diamante-joins-top-seo-creators-on-the-edward-show-for-deep-dive-into-seo-and-ai-marketing)).
- **This is a much bigger, longer-established public footprint than Zach's.**

**The "Compact Keywords" method** ([official page](https://edwardsturm.com/compact-keywords/); summaries):
- Target **low-volume, high-buyer-intent** searches people type right before buying. For us that means pages like "[service] in [suburb]," "[service] cost [city]" or "emergency [service] near [area]."
- Answer each one with a **short page** (secondary summaries say about 200–500 words) instead of long blog posts. Use internal links and track results.
- The pitch is that new, low-authority sites can rank for these.
- **Fit with our plan:** this is the **organic website** lever the main report mentions (§2, §6). It works alongside one Google Business Profile, and it's the main lever for Option 4 (honest website-only lead gen). It doesn't need extra profiles.

**Claims to verify:**
- **The pressure-washing testimonial on his own page.** A local owner says they'd spent about $18K on agencies with little result. After the course they say they now outrank "the local SEO agency who also runs a pressure washing company," get about 6–8 calls a day on good days, and did about **$100K of business from SEO in year one**. Self-reported, on the seller's own page.
- "Rankings maintained for four years without updates."
- "87% of students saw ranking gains in six weeks." I found no primary source for this.
- His Microsoft and Time roles.

**Price:**
- Not confirmed from the official page.
- **Warning:** search results are full of "reseller" sites offering the course for $20–$50 (marked down from "$499" or "$997"). These look like **pirated-course sites**. Avoid them: they're likely copyright infringement, they may be scams, and they won't have updates. If we buy, buy from edwardsturm.com.

**Getting his content into text (easiest first):**
1. **YouTube (The Edward Show and his short videos):** use the built-in "Show transcript" button, or `yt-dlp --write-auto-subs --skip-download <url>` locally. Long-form episodes are the richest material.
2. **Instagram reels:** use the [transcription script](tools/ig_transcribe.py). Prioritize the local-services reels, such as the SparkleMaids service-pages reel, over general growth content like the Airbnb or AI-detector reels.
3. **The newsletter:** it already exists as text, so subscribe and archive the relevant issues.

**What to pull out:**
- How he structures pages for each service and each service area for a local business.
- How he finds compact keywords for home services.
- How fast new pages rank.
- His view on Google Business Profiles versus websites.
- Anything on AI search visibility (45% of consumers now use AI tools for local recommendations, per the main report §6).
